Peptide Tracker

A dose log, vial tracker and mixing calculator for iPhone and iPad.

See what is due today, mark each dose as taken, and know how much is left in every vial. It is free, there is no account, and your log stays on your device.

What it does

  • Shows the doses due today from the plan you set, with a reminder if you want one
  • Records each dose with its amount, time and injection site, and lets you edit, back-date or delete it
  • Keeps a calendar and a list of every past dose, and shows the ones you missed
  • Tracks each vial: doses left and the day it runs out
  • Converts a dose into the mark on your syringe from the vial amount and the water you added
  • Exports every dose as a spreadsheet file

What it does not do

The app records what you take. It does not give medical advice, does not suggest a dose and does not sell or recommend any product. Follow the directions of your doctor or pharmacist.
