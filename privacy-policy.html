Privacy Policy

Peptide Tracker, the dose log and vial tracker app.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Your log stays on your device. Peptide Tracker has no account and no server. What you record is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your protocols, the doses you log (name, amount, time, injection site and notes), your vials, your supplies and your settings. These are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • The reminders you choose are scheduled on your device. They are local notifications; no push server is involved.
  • If you add the app's widgets, the app keeps a short summary for them on your device: the doses that are due next and how many doses each vial has left. A dose you mark as taken on a widget or on a reminder is held there too, until the app next opens and writes it into your log. The summary is shared only between the app and its own widgets.

Widgets and reminders on your screen

A widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen shows the name, amount and time of your next doses, or the name of a vial with the doses left and the day it runs out. A reminder shows the name and amount of the dose. Anyone who can see your screen can read them. If you switch on the lock in the app's Settings, widgets and reminders say "Dose" and "Vial" instead of the names you typed, and leave out the amount of a dose.

The lock

You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The check is done by your iPhone or iPad. The app is only told whether it passed; it never sees your face, your fingerprint or your passcode.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us. If you export your doses or save a backup, the file goes only where you choose to send it, through the share sheet: to Files, AirDrop, Mail or another app you pick. A backup holds your whole log. If you set a password, the file is encrypted with it before it leaves the app, and it cannot be opened without that password, by us or by anyone else. Without a password, anyone who has the file can read it. We never receive your backups and cannot recover a lost file or a forgotten password.

Usage information, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising identifier, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites. We have no way to know who uses the app or what they record.

Deleting your data

Delete a dose, a protocol or a vial in the app and it is gone from the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device, so save a backup first if you want a copy. Backup and export files you have saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.
