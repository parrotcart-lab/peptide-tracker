Privacy Policy

Peptide Tracker, the dose log and vial tracker app.

Last updated 8 October 2026

Your log stays on your device. Peptide Tracker has no account and no server. What you record is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your protocols, the doses you log (name, amount, time, injection site and notes), your vials, your supplies and your settings. These are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • The reminders you choose are scheduled on your device. They are local notifications; no push server is involved.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us. If you export your doses, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

Usage information, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no advertising identifier, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites. We have no way to know who uses the app or what they record.

Deleting your data

Delete a dose, a protocol or a vial in the app and it is gone from the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device, so export your doses first if you want a copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.
