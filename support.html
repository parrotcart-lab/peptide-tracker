Adding what you take

Open Protocols and tap New. Give it a name, the amount, how often and the time. Its doses then appear on Today.

Fixing a dose

Tap Edit next to any dose, on Today or in History, to change its amount, date, time, site or note, or to delete it. To add one you forgot, pick the day in History and tap Add a missed dose.

Vials and doses left

Add a vial in Vials with the same name as its protocol, then mix it by entering the water you added. Each dose you mark as taken comes off that vial, and the app shows the day it runs out.

The calculator

Enter the amount in the vial, the water you added and the dose you want. The answer is the mark to draw to on your syringe. It converts units only and does not suggest a dose.

Reminders

Each protocol has its own reminder switch, and Settings has one for all of them. If no reminder arrives, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your iPhone's Settings.

Keeping a copy of your log

In Settings or History, tap Export. Your log lives only on your device, so export it before deleting the app.

Medical questions

The app records what you take and does not give medical advice. Please ask your doctor or pharmacist.
